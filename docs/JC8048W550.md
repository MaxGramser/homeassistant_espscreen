# Guition JC8048W550C_I, 5 inch, experimental

This is the **JC8048W550C_I**: a 5-inch 800 x 480 ST7262 panel over RGB, GT911 capacitive touch, and an
ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 N16R8 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM. Only the capacitive variant: the **JC8048W550N_I** has
no touch and the **JC8048W550R_I** a resistive panel, and neither is this board file.

It has run on a real board: picture, touch, the backlight and the connection to Home Assistant work. Physical
acceptance by the project has not been performed.

## Install

Update Tessera Screen Manager and choose **Guition · 5 inch** (JC8048W550C_I, marked Experimental) in **New screen**.
Follow [Easy setup](EASY_SETUP.md) to create a profile with its own name, Wi-Fi references and unique API/OTA keys.
Choose the correct USB port, or download the firmware to flash with ESPHome Web from your own computer.
Keep an existing working profile if the board is already installed. Tessera adds it to Home Assistant by itself once it
is on your Wi-Fi; then add its tiles.

The remote package is `packages/jc8048w550ci.yaml`; the checkout entry is `checkout/jc8048w550ci.yaml`. Both combine
`packages/core.yaml` with `packages/boards/guition-jc8048w550c-i.yaml`, which takes the ESP32-S3 and its PSRAM from
`packages/hardware/esp32s3-rgb.yaml`.

## Layout and capabilities

- The standard look at 186.6 dpi (933 diagonal pixels over 5 inches), the density of the Waveshare 5-inch, so a tile
  keeps its size in millimetres. A screen starts with three columns of three cells lying down and one column of five
  standing up, and the editor gives it another grid within what the glass holds.
- The backlight is a PWM pin (GPIO2) at 150 Hz, where this board's driver keeps its brightness, with a gamma of 2.0:
  brightness is a percentage, and standby, night mode, Sleep, Wake and the alert's flashes are enabled.
- Camera tiles, full-screen snapshots, live tile pictures, camera alerts and media artwork use the shared PSRAM
  implementation.
- The GT911 reports pixel coordinates, with no calibration. The existing touch filter and action guard stay in use.
- LVGL uses a 12 % draw buffer, as the other 800 x 480 boards do.

## Hardware references

- The RGB pins and timings follow [baget/JC8048W550-ESPHome](https://github.com/baget/JC8048W550-ESPHome). The red
  and blue groups are exchanged against that mapping, with `color_order: BGR` and `byte_order: little_endian`, which
  is what gives this bus neutral greys.
- I2C for the GT911: SDA GPIO19, SCL GPIO20, touch reset GPIO38.

## What to report while testing

1. A successful boot and appearance in Tessera.
2. Correct colours and a stable picture across several page changes, a restart, an update over the air and a cold
   start.
3. Taps near each corner, slider drags and edge swipes, lying down and standing up.
4. The brightness setting across its range, standby after the timeout and the wake by touch, and night mode.
5. A full page of tiles, camera pictures and camera alerts.
6. `sensor.<screen>_heap_free` with a full layout, and any reset or Wi-Fi drop.

Do not publish Wi-Fi passwords or API/OTA keys with logs.
