# Guition JC4827W543, 4.3 inch, 480 × 272 (experimental)

A 4.3-inch board from Guition (**JC4827W543**, also sold as **JC4827W543C** with a capacitive touch panel and as the
Sunton 4827S043): a 480 x 272 panel with an **NV3041A** driver on a four-line QSPI bus and **GT911 capacitive
touch**, on an ESP32-S3 with 4 MB flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM. It is a 4 MB board like the CYD, so it builds with the
wide update slots (`packages/hardware/flash-4mb.yaml`) and the compact look.

The board is **experimental**: its file was built from ESPHome's own model for this board (`model: JC4827W543`) and the
community configuration for it (devices.esphome.io). It builds, flashes and boots on a real unit and shows the
"Waiting for Home Assistant" screen. Touch was tried on the glass and taps land where they should, with the
touch direction below. Not checked on a unit yet: the backlight levels, a screen standing up and the free memory with
a full layout. If you have one,
[tell others how it went](https://tessera-maxgramser.on-forge.com/community/share?type=installation&board=jc4827w543).

## Install

Update Tessera Screen Manager and choose **Guition · 4.3 inch** (JC4827W543) in **New screen**. Follow
[Easy setup](EASY_SETUP.md) to create a profile with its own name, Wi-Fi references and unique API/OTA keys, then
flash it over USB or download the firmware for ESPHome Web. There is no touch calibration: the touch panel reports
pixels.

The remote package is `packages/jc4827w543.yaml`; the checkout entry is `checkout/jc4827w543.yaml`. Both combine
`packages/core.yaml` with `packages/boards/jc4827w543.yaml`.

This board needs ESPHome 2026.9.0 or later (`min_version` in its hardware file): on older versions the firmware is
larger than the update slot ([Updating 4 MB screens](UPDATING_4MB_SCREENS.md)).

## Layout and capabilities

- Lying down: 480 x 272, three columns of four cells. Standing up: 272 x 480, two columns of eight.
- The compact look at 128 dpi (552 diagonal pixels over 4.3 inches).
- Capacitive touch with an interrupt line, no polling.
- The backlight is a PWM pin (GPIO1) at 1000 Hz: dimming, standby, night and an alert's flashes are meant to work.
- 128 tiles on 24 pages, from the PSRAM. No camera pictures: the board has no camera, and the 4 MB firmware leaves no
  room for the snapshot package.
- LVGL draws through a 25 % buffer. Read `sensor.<screen>_heap_free` with a full layout; under 40 KB is too little.

## Pins

| Part | Pins |
| --- | --- |
| Display bus (QSPI) | CLK GPIO47, data GPIO21, GPIO48, GPIO40, GPIO39; CS GPIO45 (from ESPHome's model) |
| Touch (GT911, I2C) | SDA GPIO8, SCL GPIO4, interrupt GPIO3 (a strapping pin), reset GPIO38 |
| Backlight | GPIO1 |

The 3.5-inch Guition has the touch's data and clock lines the other way round.

## Touch direction

The GT911 on this board reads its glass a half turn against the panel's own scan. The board file therefore turns the
picture a half turn on the display (`transform: mirror_x, mirror_y`), which makes the picture and the touch agree. It was
measured by the community configuration's author by tapping the page keys, and taps land correctly on a second unit.
