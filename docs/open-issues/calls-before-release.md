# Calls: what is left before a release

- **Flash on 4 MB boards.** The call's screen texts are in every board's English table, also where the module is not built. The CYD is at
  96.2 % of its slot (1,953,999 bytes, up 1,344). `cyd9342` and `hosyond40` have not been built against the 4 MB budget.
- **The ring's volume as a setting.** The ring stands at the speaker's top while it rings; it should be a setting in a Calls group of the
  screen's settings (docs/SETTINGS.md). The doorbell's chime has only the screen's own volume, and is as soft as a call's ring was.
- **Ringing at night.** A screen in standby rings at full volume. Whether a screen should have quiet hours beside the switch Call ringtone is
  a question for the owner.
- **Measured on two boards only**, a reTerminal D1001 and a Guition 10.1. The ring's loudness through the speaker's own volume has not been
  heard: the ring was off when it was made. Voice switching has not been judged by ear. Hardware echo references (docs/CALLS.md, "Echo") and
  Do not disturb, the Calls settings and the editor's Calls card are not built.
