# GUITION JC1060F570C_I_W_Y (ESP32-S31, 7 inch, 1024 × 600)

Status: **experimental**. Built and checked with `esphome config` on ESPHome 2026.9.0. Not yet tried on glass by the project.

The first ESP32-S31 board in Tessera. Board key `jc1060f570c`, board file `packages/boards/jc1060f570c.yaml`, hardware
in `packages/hardware/guition-jc1060f570c.yaml` and the chip in `packages/hardware/esp32s31-rgb.yaml`.

## Hardware

| Part | What it is | Tessera |
|---|---|---|
| Module | ESP32-S31-WROOM-3-N16R16V: 320 MHz RISC-V, 16 MB flash, 16 MB octal PSRAM | `variant: esp32s31`, 320 MHz, PSRAM octal at 200 MHz, ESP-IDF 6.1.1 |
| Panel | 7.0-inch IPS, 1024 × 600, JD9165, 16-bit RGB565 | `mipi_rgb` model `RPI` (no init sequence), 34 MHz pixel clock |
| Touch | GT911 on I2C (SDA GPIO2, SCL GPIO3), no reset or interrupt line | `hardware/parts/gt911-polled.yaml`, polled every 20 ms |
| Backlight | PWM on GPIO52 | LEDC at 20 kHz, dimmable |
| Audio | ES8389 codec (I2C 0x20, I2S BCLK 54, LRCK 47, DOUT 56, DIN 16), NS4150 amplifiers (enable GPIO42) | **not driven**: ESPHome has no ES8389 driver. The amplifier enable is held low |
| Ethernet | IP101 over RMII (CLK in 13, TX_EN 12, TXD0/1 8/9, CRS_DV 15, RXD0/1 19/18, MDC 53, MDIO 10, reset 17) | **not driven**: ESPHome 2026.9 takes RMII PHYs only on the ESP32 and ESP32-P4, and on the S31 only RGMII ones |
| Log and flashing | the chip's USB Serial/JTAG, on the full-speed USB-C port | `hardware_uart: USB_SERIAL_JTAG` |

The pins and timings come from GUITION's own board package,
[`components/board/guition_boards/jc1060f570c`](https://github.com/Guition-JC/Guition/tree/main/components/board/guition_boards/jc1060f570c),
and the Ethernet pins from its factory demo's `main/Kconfig.projbuild`. In places the package's comments name other nets
than its values (they look copied from a sibling board). The values are what the factory firmware runs, and they are what
Tessera uses. The backlight is on GPIO52, as `guition_boards` 0.2.1 corrected it. GPIO17, the pin it had before, is the
Ethernet PHY's reset.

## What to check on the glass

1. **Colours**: red and blue the right way round. If they are swapped, swap the `red` and `blue` lists under
   `data_pins` in `packages/hardware/guition-jc1060f570c.yaml`. GUITION's package says `LCD_RGB_ELEMENT_ORDER_BGR`, but
   ESP-IDF's RGB panel driver takes the data lines in D0–D15 order whatever that says, and that order is what is wired here.
2. **Touch**: all four corners and a drag. The GT911 reports panel pixels; if a corner is mirrored, the chip's own
   configuration disagrees with the glass and needs a `transform:` in the screen's Override YAML.
3. **A steady picture under load**: open a big layout, a camera view and a page switch, while Wi-Fi is busy. See below.
4. **Backlight**: dims smoothly from 1 % to 100 % and goes dark in standby.

## A known risk: the picture can slip

[espressif/esp-idf#19395](https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/issues/19395) reports this very board: with the
framebuffer in PSRAM and a bounce buffer, a moment of PSRAM contention (a heavy LVGL redraw or Wi-Fi traffic) can shift
the picture sideways or vertically. The shift stays until the panel is reset. The ESP32-S3 has a recovery path for this
in ESP-IDF that the S31 does not have yet. ESPHome's `mipi_rgb` always uses a ten-line bounce buffer, and the board file
cannot change that. Espressif suggested turning the bounce buffer off on the S31. The mitigations here are the 200 MHz
PSRAM and an LVGL draw buffer in PSRAM (`LVGL_BUFFER_SIZE: 25%`). If the picture slips on glass, report it with the build
log and what was on the screen.

## Not yet

- No standby key. The S31's boot strapping pins are GPIO60 and GPIO61, and which one the BOOT button reaches is not
  confirmed from the schematic.
- No speaker or microphones until ESPHome drives the ES8389.
- No Ethernet until ESPHome takes an RMII PHY on the S31.
- The RX8025T RTC and the microSD slot are left alone. The screen takes its time from Home Assistant.
